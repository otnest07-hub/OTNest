Your first visit

  1. Message or call. Choose a clinic, a home visit or a video call, and a time that suits you.
  2. Assessment. Dr. Satish listens, checks how you move and explains what’s going on.
  3. Treatment starts — usually in the same session.
  4. Your plan. Home exercises, a realistic timeline and how many sessions to expect.

Bring any scans or reports, and wear clothes you can move in.

Answers to Your Most Common Questions About Physiotherapy Care and Our Services

Make A Call
How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition — most patients see real improvement in 6 to 10 sessions. We reassess progress regularly and adjust the plan as you recover.
Do you offer emergency physiotherapy sessions?

Yes, we hold same-day slots every morning for acute pain, sports injuries, or post-surgical complications that need prompt attention.
Are your treatments painful?

Most sessions are dosed to your tolerance and stay within a pain-free range — techniques like dry needling are the exception, and we always explain them first.
What conditions do you treat?

We treat sports injuries, post-surgical recovery, chronic back and neck pain, neurological conditions, and paediatric and geriatric mobility issues — see our Services page for the full list.
Do you accept UPI and cards?

You can pay by cash or UPI. Fees are kept fair and we always tell you the full cost before starting treatment.
