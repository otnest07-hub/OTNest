Home visits · Patna & Gurgaon

Physiotherapy and occupational therapy at home

Some recoveries are easier at home — and some are only possible there. Dr. Satish visits patients across Patna and Gurgaon who can’t easily travel, bringing the same assessment and treatment you’d get at the clinic.

Who home visits are for

  • After a knee or hip replacement, or any surgery that makes stairs and car journeys hard.
  • After a stroke, or with Parkinson’s or another neurological condition.
  • Older adults who feel unsteady, have had a fall, or are recovering from an illness.
  • Severe back or neck flare-ups, where sitting in a car is the problem.
  • Children who settle better in their own space for occupational therapy.

What a home visit looks like

  1. The same assessment as the clinic — history, movement, strength and balance.
  2. A safety look around the home. Stairs, bathroom, bed height, rugs that slip, where you sit most. Small changes here often prevent the next fall.
  3. Treatment using what’s around you — a chair, a step, a towel — plus any equipment Dr. Satish brings along.
  4. Family and carers shown how to help safely with transfers and exercises between visits.
Home visits aren’t emergency care. For chest pain, sudden weakness on one side, slurred speech or a serious fall, call 108 or 112 straight away.

Getting ready for the visit

  • A little clear floor space and a sturdy chair.
  • Comfortable clothes the patient can move in.
  • Recent reports, scans or the hospital discharge summary, and a list of medicines.
  • Someone at home for the first visit if the patient is frail.

Areas we cover

Across Patna and Gurgaon. If you’re near the edge of either city, message us your area and we’ll confirm.

Fees

Home-visit fees depend on your area and the treatment needed. We’ll confirm the fee on WhatsApp before booking — no surprises.
